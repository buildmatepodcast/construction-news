ENR 22 June 2026
NIST Final Report: Design Deficiencies, Corrosion & Construction Deviations Caused Surfside Condo Collapse
The National Institute of Standards and Technology released its long-awaited final technical findings on June 22, concluding that design deficiencies, construction deviations, added loads, and long-term corrosion collectively triggered pool-deck punching-shear failures that initiated the catastrophic 2021 collapse of Champlain Towers South, killing 98 people. NIST ruled out sinkholes, nearby construction vibration, and hurricane effects — identifying long-term corrosion degradation as the likely tipping factor, with findings now underpinning Florida's landmark post-Surfside condominium inspection reforms.
