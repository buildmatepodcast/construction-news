Contractors' Backlog Jumped in September as Profit Outlook Fell

Construction backlog rose for a second straight month in September, reaching 8.7 months industry-wide. Even so, nearly a third of contractors surveyed in October expect profit margins to decline over the next 12 months, up from 26% in July.

Iran Conflict Pushes Construction Prices to Near 4-Year High

Construction material prices climbed 11.2% year-over-year in September, driven by higher fuel costs and tariff impacts tied to the Iran conflict. Lengthened lead times for steel components are making it harder for contractors to hold project schedules.
