Contractors' Backlog Jumped in September as Profit Outlook Fell

Construction backlog increased for the second consecutive month in September, with total backlog jumping to 8.7 months. About 32% of contractors surveyed expect profit margins to decline over the next 12 months, up from 26% in July.

Construction's Winners and Losers Drift Farther Apart

The gap between construction industry winners and losers is widening, with projects valued above $1 billion — many of them data centres — consuming the bulk of nonresidential construction spending. Smaller contractors are increasingly squeezed out of that growth.

Design Museum Gent Reopens with New Wing by Carmody Groarke and ATAMA

Belgium's largest design museum reopened on October 3 after a revamp led by Carmody Groarke with local firm ATAMA, adding a five-storey wing that is Belgium's tallest public timber-structured building.
